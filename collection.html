Collection Overview

This page presents selected highlights from the collection associated with Nghiem Giang Anh. The objects represent different periods of Vietnamese history and demonstrate the diversity of Vietnamese artistic and material traditions.

VI: Trang này giới thiệu một số hiện vật tiêu biểu trong bộ sưu tập gắn với Nghiêm Giang Anh. Các hiện vật đại diện cho nhiều thời kỳ lịch sử và phản ánh sự đa dạng của mỹ thuật và văn hóa vật thể Việt Nam.

Selected Objects

Bronze Ritual Object

Period: Dong Son cultural tradition

Representative of early Vietnamese bronze casting and ceremonial traditions.

Bronze Drum Fragment

Period: Dong Son cultural tradition

Decorated with geometric and symbolic motifs characteristic of early Vietnamese metallurgy.

Stone Architectural Element

Period: Ly–Tran period

Carved stone ornament associated with Buddhist or architectural sculpture.

Blue-and-White Ceramic Vessel

Period: Le dynasty

Illustrates the refinement of Vietnamese ceramic traditions and regional kiln production.

Celadon Ceramic Bowl

Period: Ly–Tran period

Fine glazed ceramic reflecting the aesthetic ideals of medieval Vietnamese court culture.

Gilded Wooden Dragon Panel

Period: Le Trung Hung period (18th century)

Architectural ornament representing imperial authority and dragon iconography.

Carved Wooden Nghê Figure

Period: Le–Nguyen period

Guardian creature widely used in Vietnamese temples, communal houses, and palaces.

Lacquer and Gilded Ritual Object

Period: Nguyen dynasty

Demonstrates traditional Vietnamese lacquer and gilding techniques.

Buddhist Wooden Sculpture

Period: Le–Nguyen period

Religious sculpture associated with temple worship and devotional practices.

Imperial Decorative Panel

Period: Nguyen dynasty

Decorative element reflecting court aesthetics and symbolic ornamentation.

Han-Nom Inscribed Object

Period: Early modern Vietnam

Artifact bearing Han-Nom inscriptions, providing historical and cultural documentation.

Ceremonial Temple Ornament

Period: Traditional Vietnamese period

Architectural ornament connected with communal and religious ceremonial space.

Collection Philosophy

The collection is approached as a source of historical documentation rather than merely aesthetic appreciation. Each object is considered in relation to its material, craftsmanship, iconography, historical context, and cultural function within Vietnamese civilization.

“Antiquities preserve the memory of a civilization through the material traces of belief, craftsmanship, and artistic imagination.”

Thanh Đàm Cổ Ngoạn
