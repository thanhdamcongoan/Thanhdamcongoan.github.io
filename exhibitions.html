Exhibitions & Museum Collaborations

Selected exhibitions, museum collaborations, and heritage preservation activities involving artifacts and research associated with Nghiem Giang Anh.

2023

Long Vân Khánh Hội – The Dragon in Vietnamese Culture

Venue: Ho Chi Minh City Museum of History

Selected objects from the collection were presented in a major exhibition exploring the iconography of the dragon in Vietnamese art and material culture.

2024

From the Red River Civilization to Hanoi Streets

Venue: Hanoi Museum

Artifacts from the collection were exhibited in a commemorative exhibition celebrating the 70th anniversary of the Liberation of Hanoi, highlighting the historical continuity of Vietnamese civilization and urban culture.

2023

Centenary of the Hue Museum of Royal Antiquities (1923–2023)

Venue: Hue Museum of Royal Antiquities

Participation in commemorative heritage activities and museum collaboration programs marking the museum’s 100th anniversary.

Heritage Preservation

As part of his long-term commitment to Vietnamese cultural heritage, Nghiem Giang Anh has donated selected artifacts and historical materials from his private collection to cultural institutions in Vietnam.

Hue Museum of Royal Antiquities

Donation of selected antiquities from the private collection.

Tang Tho Lau

Donation of rare books and historical documents to the Hue Monuments Conservation Centre.

Ho Chi Minh City Museum of History

Collaboration through exhibitions and cultural heritage activities.

Archaeological and Cultural Activities

Nghiem Giang Anh has participated in archaeological and cultural heritage activities related to the annual archaeological research programs of the Institute of Archaeology of Vietnam, contributing to the documentation and dissemination of Vietnamese historical and cultural heritage.

“Cultural heritage is preserved not only through museums and monuments, but also through research, publication, public education, and responsible collecting.”

Thanh Đàm Cổ Ngoạn
